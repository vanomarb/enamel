New patients

What happens at your first visit, the HMO paperwork we take care of, how to pay, and answers to common questions. Prices are starting prices.

What happens at your first visit

A first visit is a check-up and consultation: ₱500 and about 30 minutes. You can add a cleaning to the same visit for ₱1,000 and another 45 minutes.

Book a check-up

  1. On arrival

    Check in

    Fill in a short patient form. If you’re using an HMO, hand over your card and ID.

  2. About 30 minutes

    Check-up and consultation

    The dentist checks your teeth and gums. X-rays only if they’re needed, at ₱400 each.

  3. Before any treatment

    Your plan, with prices

    You get each treatment and its price in writing. Nothing starts until you say yes.

  4. At the end

    Book what’s next

    Book your next visit before you leave. We’ll remind you about your check-up in six months.

HMO cards and payment

We accept HMO cards. What’s covered depends on your plan, so we request a letter of authorization (LOA) from your HMO before any covered procedure.

HMO checklist

  • Bring your HMO card The physical card, or a digital card if your HMO issues one.
  • Bring one valid ID Any government-issued ID with your photo, so we can match it to your card.
  • We request the LOA Before a covered procedure, we ask your HMO for the letter of authorization. You don’t need to.
  • Coverage depends on your plan If your plan covers only part of a treatment, we tell you the balance first.

Payment

GCash, Maya, debit or credit card, or cash.

Common questions

How much is a first visit?

A check-up and consultation starts at ₱500 and takes about 30 minutes. X-rays are ₱400 each, only if the dentist needs one. You get the plan and prices in writing before any treatment.

Do you accept HMO cards?

Yes. Bring your HMO card and one valid ID. We request the letter of authorization (LOA) from your HMO before covered procedures, and what’s covered depends on your plan.

Can I pay for braces in installments?

Yes. Metal braces are a ₱45,000 package: ₱9,000 down, then ₱1,500 a month for 24 months. Ceramic braces are a ₱60,000 package, also in installments.

When is the orthodontist in?

On Saturdays only, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Braces consultations, fittings and monthly adjustments are all booked on Saturdays.

Do you see children?

Yes. Children see the same general dentists, and fluoride for kids starts at ₱600 and takes about 20 minutes.

Which time zone are the booking times in?

Cebu time (UTC+8). If you’re booking from abroad, for yourself or for family at home, the days and times you see are the clinic’s, not your phone’s.
