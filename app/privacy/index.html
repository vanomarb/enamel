Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information, following the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its implementing rules and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

Last updated 18 November 2026

This is a demonstration site

Nothing you enter on this site is sent to us or to anyone else. The booking, the sign-in, the newsletter and the contact form work only inside your browser. There are no accounts on a server, no analytics and no advertising.

What this site keeps, and where

Two items are kept in your browser’s local storage, on your device only, and only after you use the feature that needs them:

  • Your sign-in: the first name and the email address or mobile number you entered, so the site can greet you and show your appointments.
  • Your appointments: the treatment, dentist, day, time, patient name and HMO provider (if you entered one) of each booking you confirm, so My appointments can list them.

Signing out removes the first item. You can remove appointments one by one in My appointments, or choose “Remove everything saved here” there to clear both items at once, with a confirmation first. Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings does the same. The Cookie Policy lists the exact names.

Health information

A dental clinic handles health information, which the Data Privacy Act treats as sensitive personal information. A clinic may collect it only for your care, with your consent or as the law allows, keep it confidential, share it only with those who need it (such as your HMO for an LOA, with your agreement), and keep it secure. This site collects none: your dental history is never asked for here.

What a clinic visit would involve

At a real visit, a clinic would ask for your name, contact details, date of birth, HMO details if you use one, and your dental and medical history, and would keep your records for as long as the law and good dental practice require.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to object, to access your information, to have it corrected, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Security

The site loads no third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself. What it keeps never leaves your device.

Contact

To reach our Data Protection Officer, use the contact form and choose “Privacy”. As this is a demonstration site, messages are not sent.
