About Enamel

A family dental clinic in Guadalupe, Cebu City, with three chairs, two general dentists and an orthodontist on Saturdays.

Why the prices are on the page

Not knowing what a visit will cost keeps a lot of people away from the dentist. So every treatment on this site shows its starting price, its time in the chair and what’s included, and the dentist gives you your plan in writing before any work starts.

How we work

  • Information first. You see the price, the time and the number of visits before you book.
  • One thing at a time. A check-up first, then a plan, then treatment when you say yes.
  • HMO paperwork handled. Bring your card and one valid ID; we request the letter of authorization for you.
  • The whole family. Children see the same general dentists, and the orthodontist is in on Saturdays for braces.

Visit us

We’re open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, in Guadalupe, Cebu City. See the dentists and hours or book a visit.
