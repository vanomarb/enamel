Cookie Policy

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It keeps two essential items in your browser’s local storage, and only when you use the feature that needs them.

Last updated 18 November 2026

What the site keeps

enamel.account
Your first name and the email address or mobile number you signed in with. Set when you sign in; removed when you sign out.
enamel.appointments
The bookings you confirm on this device: treatment, dentist, day, time, patient name and the HMO provider if you entered one. Set when you confirm a booking; remove each one in My appointments.

Both are essential: without them, the sign-in and My appointments can’t work. They never leave your device, and nothing is read by anyone else.

No tracking

There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party scripts. The fonts and icons are part of the site.

Clearing what’s kept

Sign out to remove your sign-in, remove appointments one by one in My appointments, or choose “Remove everything saved here” in My appointments to clear both items at once (it asks first, and it can’t be undone). You can also clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings. If your browser blocks storage, the site still works; it just won’t remember you.

This is a demonstration site

Enamel is a concept brand. This is a demonstration site: nothing you enter here is sent, and no payments are taken.
